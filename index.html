The Story

A forest that doesn't want you here.

You wake up in the remnants of a crashed bush plane, surrounded by towering pines and an unnatural fog that creeps between the trunks. Your supplies are scattered. Your radio is dead. And something — several somethings — is moving in the trees.

Wild Forest is a single-player survival sandbox where the wilderness is both your provider and your enemy. Chop wood, build shelter, craft weapons, hunt for food, and explore a massive procedurally generated forest that changes with every new game.

  • ✓ No hand-holding. No minimap. Just instinct.
  • ✓ Day-night cycle with dynamic weather and seasons.
  • ✓ Build everything from a lean-to to a fortified cabin.
Deep Forest Biome
Game Features

Built for survivors, not tourists.

Infinite Procedural World

Every playthrough generates a unique forest — new rivers, caves, ruins, and creature dens. No two runs are ever the same.

Deep Crafting System

Over 200 craftable items — from stone axes to compound bows to camouflaged traps. Tier up your tools as you push deeper into the wild.

Build & Fortify

Construct shelters piece by piece. Reinforce walls, set alarms, light fires — the creatures hunt at night, and your base is the only safe haven.

Dynamic Survival Needs

Hunger, thirst, warmth, stamina, and sanity — five interlocking systems that make every decision matter. Eat the wrong berry and the forest gets a new ghost.

Living Ecosystem

Deer graze, wolves pack-hunt, fish swim downstream. Every animal has its own AI behaviour — observe and exploit the food chain, or become part of it.

Hidden Lore

Caves, abandoned camps, and ancient shrines hide fragments of a story the forest has buried. Piece it together to unlock the true ending.
